## Bypass Sliding Door

The **Bypass Sliding Door** provides smooth and silent gliding performance thanks to its four precision top rollers and stainless steel top track, preserving a completely clean, frameless aesthetic.

### Why Specify It

- Reversible double sliding configuration utilizing 3/8" (10 mm) tempered glass.
- Includes Easy Clean glass coating for effortless daily maintenance against water spots and soap residue.
- International safety certification SGCC ANSI Z7.1.
- Ergonomic 25×13 mm stainless steel handles.

### Typical Applications

Residential master baths, luxury condominium towers, and hospitality applications requiring optimized enclosure access without compromising contemporary design.
