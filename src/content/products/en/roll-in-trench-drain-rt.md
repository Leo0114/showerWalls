## Roll-In Trench Drain

**Roll-In Trench Drain** (code `RT`) arrives pre-sloped, leak-tested and ready to set. Because it is fabricated around your drain location, there is no field pitching, no mud bed and no waiting for a mortar cure.

- Slip-resistant textured floor
- Matching threshold or curbless transition where required
