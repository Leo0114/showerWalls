## Free Standing Tub

The **Free Standing Tub** (code `BT`) is our made-to-order freestanding tub, fabricated in the same solid surface as the wall system so the whole bathroom reads as one material.

- Built to the dimensions and wall thickness your layout allows
- Warm-to-the-touch, non-porous surface that holds heat
- Repairable finish — scratches sand out instead of chipping
- Coordinated with the surrounding wall pattern and accessories
