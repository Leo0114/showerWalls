## Wall-Mounted Soap Dish

The **Wall-Mounted Soap Dish** (code `WM`) is fabricated in the same solid surface as the walls, so it reads as part of the shower rather than an add-on bolted to it. It ships with the panel package and is installed by the same crew.

### Details

- Seamless, non-porous joint with the surrounding panel
- Available in all five standard finishes
- Wipe-clean geometry with no grout lines or silicone traps
- Positioning coordinated during the submittal stage

### Good to know

Accessory placement is set before fabrication. Send us the elevation and we will mark the layout on the shop drawing for approval.
