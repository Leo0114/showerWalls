## Custom Pan

**Custom Pan** (code `CP`) arrives pre-sloped, leak-tested and ready to set. Because it is fabricated around your drain location, there is no field pitching, no mud bed and no waiting for a mortar cure.

### Included

- Integrated pre-sloped base with a factory-set drain position
- Reinforced solid-surface construction rated for commercial traffic
- Slip-resistant textured floor
- Matching threshold or curbless transition where required

### Accessibility

Roll-in, curbless and full ADA configurations are available across the family — tell us the clearance you need to meet and we will build the pan to it.
