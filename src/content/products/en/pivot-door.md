## Pivot Door with one Fixed Panel

The **Pivot Door** from the _Frameless Collection_ combines sleek minimalism with maximum functionality. It features a reversible pivot opening system alongside a sturdy 10 mm high-safety tempered glass fixed panel.

### Why Specify It

- SGCC Certified ANSI Z7.1 3/8" (10 mm) tempered glass with factory-included Easy Clean glass coating.
- High-grade stainless steel wall clamps, 25×13 mm handle, and aluminum wall-mounted profile with a curved bottom threshold.
- Reversible design adaptable to any layout configuration.
- Optimized standard sizing and robust packaging options (Caja / Huacal).

### Typical Applications

Ideal for high-end residential projects, hospitality developments, and modern bathroom renovations seeking visual spaciousness without heavy framing obstructions.
