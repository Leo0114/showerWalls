## The brief

Ninety-six shared bathrooms, one summer break, and a hard move-in date that could not slip. Student housing also punishes a bathroom harder than any other sector, so the finish had to survive a decade of it.

## What we did

Fabrication ran in parallel with the end of the semester, so panels were on site the week the building emptied. Trench drain pans were specified for their drying speed — a shared bathroom used by four students never gets an idle hour.

Accessories were kept flush and integrated: nothing bolted on, nothing to pull off the wall.

## The result

- All 96 suites completed in 10 weeks, ahead of move-in
- No grout lines, no silicone joints and no visible fasteners
- Non-porous surfaces that clean with a single wipe
- Maintenance calls in the first year down to almost none
