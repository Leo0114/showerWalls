## The brief

A 168-unit repositioning with leases already signed against the turnover calendar. Every apartment had to look the same on handover, and the schedule allowed three days per unit including paint.

## What we did

Half the building mirrors the other half, so we specified reversible drain pans and fabricated a single wall package that works in both orientations. That cut the SKU count in half and removed the risk of a crew fitting a left-hand pan in a right-hand bathroom.

Forty ground-floor units were converted from tub to shower in the same pass, reusing the existing drain location.

## The result

- 168 identical bathrooms with no visible variation between units
- Two days per unit, one ahead of the allowance
- Tub-to-shower conversions with no plumbing relocation
- No grout to re-seal between tenancies
