## The brief

Harbor Point needed to refresh 214 guest bathrooms without closing the property. Tile was ruled out on day one: the demolition noise, the dust containment and the mortar cure time meant three weeks per floor and a hotel full of complaints.

## What we did

We surveyed every rough opening across the four bathroom types on the property and fabricated panels and pans per type, not per room. Installation ran one floor per week, on a Monday-to-Thursday cycle, so every floor was back in inventory before the weekend.

Existing substrates stayed in place. Panels went straight over the old tile, which removed the demolition phase entirely and kept the corridors clean enough to stay open to guests.

## The result

- Zero guest-facing demolition and no dust containment scaffolding
- 214 bathrooms delivered in 11 weeks, three weeks ahead of the schedule
- One warranty covering walls, pans and accessories
- A grout-free surface the housekeeping team cleans in a single pass
