## The brief

Forty-eight patient bathrooms that had to pass an accessibility review before anything else. Curbless entry, wheelchair clearance and grab-bar blocking were fixed constraints, and the wards stayed occupied throughout.

## What we did

Every pan was fabricated as a curbless roll-in with a trench drain set at the far wall, so the floor slope never fights the chair. Grab-bar backing was coordinated on the shop drawing and built into the panel before it left the shop — no drilling through a finished surface on site.

Work was sequenced two rooms at a time so the ward never lost more than one bathroom per wing.

## The result

- 48 compliant roll-in showers approved on first inspection
- Curbless thresholds with no lip and no transition strip
- Grab-bar backing integrated at fabrication, not improvised on site
- A seamless surface that meets the facility's infection-control standard
